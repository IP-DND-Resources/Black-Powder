# Black Powder Explosives

#### Black Powder
Setting fire to a container full of black powder causes, it to explode. When a container explodes, each creature in a 10-foot-radius Sphere centered on the container makes a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) Fire damage (for a powder horn) or 24 (7d6) Fire damage (for a keg) on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Crafting Explosives

To craft an explosive, you need the required powder weight for the item in addition to half of the item's remaining base GP cost worth of other materials after subtracing the value of Black Powder.

It takes approximately 1 hour of intentionally focused work with either Alchemist's Supplies or Tinker's Tools to assemble an explosive if you have the required materials and are proficient with the tool.

#### Dynamite Stick
An a Utilize Action, you can light a Dynamite Stick and throw it at a point up to 60 feet away, where it explodes. Each creature in a 5-foot-radius Sphere centered on that point makes a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 Force damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. 

It takes 1 minute to bind two or more Dynamite Sticks together into a bundle so they explode at the same time. Each stick after the first increases the damage by 1d6 (to a maximum of 10d6) and the effect's radius by 5 feet (to a maximum of 20 feet).

#### Fire Pots
As a Utilize Action, you can throw a small hand sized cermaic sphere at a point up to 60 feet away, where it explodes, creating a particular effect in a 20-foot-radius Sphere. 

**Scrap Fire Pot.** Each creature in the Sphere makes a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 17 (5d6) Piercing damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. 

**Smoke Fire Pot.** The area of the Sphere is Heavily Obscured by smoke for 1 minute. A strong wind (such as the Gust of Wind spell) disperses the smoke.

**Stunning Fire Pot.** Each creature in the Sphere makes a series of two saving throws. On a failed DC 15 Constitution saving throw, a creature has the Deafened condition for 1 minute. On a failed DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, a creature has the Blinded condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of the affected creature's turns, the creature repeats each save if still affected, ending the effect on itself on a success.

#### Chaining Explosives
Dynamite and black powder, can each be rigged with a longer fuse so it explodes after a longer period of time, such as 1 minute or 10 minutes. It takes 1 minute to rig each explosive this way.
